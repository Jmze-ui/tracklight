          Choose a folder to browse. Up/Down move, Enter opens or loads, Backspace goes up, Space auditions.

          GROOVE & SWING

          One bar per row, repeating every 16 rows. A taller bar means that row lasts longer, so the next note comes later. The total stays the same, so the tempo does not change. Swing 50% is straight, 67% is a triplet shuffle.
